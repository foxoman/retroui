Retro Nim UI, live

My apps made with Retro Nim UI, running in your browser: the same code as on Windows, macOS, Linux, Android and iOS, built as WebAssembly. They keep your files in the browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

Retro AtelierPictures, drawings and pages, as in 1994: layers, filters, vector tools, books and magazines.
Control GalleryEvery control of the kit, page by page, in the Windows 3.1 look or the Glass look.
The first visit downloads the app (Retro Atelier is about 23 MB) and loads once more to turn on its threads; after that it works offline. Source and downloads: github.com/foxoman/retroui. Developed by Sultan Al Isaiee (@foxoman).
